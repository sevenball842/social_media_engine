## LEGAL FIREPOWER: THE RED FLAGS

### When to Deploy:

**Use legal red flags strategically:**

- **Early in negotiation** (now): Show that you've done homework; demonstrate legal risk
- **If she refuses to move on Tier 1**: "These legal issues + bad economics = I have to walk"
- **During counsel review**: Counsel will use case law to push back on problematic provisions
- **As LAST resort**: "If you won't move on salary/commitment/attribution, we need to at least fix these legal red flags"

### Red Flag Leverage:

```
Elizabeth's Vulnerabilities:

1. Indefinite Confidentiality
   → Likely unenforceable under NH law (no sunset)
   → Case law: Dynamics Research Corp. v. Analytic Sciences (Mass. 1993)
   → Counsel will advise: "Add 3-year sunset or risk unenforceability"
   
2. Extended Non-Compete (24-36 months)
   → May exceed NH reasonableness standard
   → Case law: Berman v. Marcus, 640 A.2d 489 (N.H. 1994) — 3 years deemed unreasonable
   → Counsel will advise: "Cap at 12 months or risk unenforceability"

3. Vest-and-Dash Clawback
   → Conflicts with your own "earned is earned" principle
   → May violate good-faith covenant (RSA 304-C:40)
   → Counsel will advise: "Remove or make mutual"

4. 90-Day Step-Down Penalty
   → Arbitrary calendar penalty, not tied to performance
   → May be unenforceable as penalty clause (not liquidated damages)
   → Counsel will advise: "Remove or face enforceability challenge"
```

**How to Use:**
- Don't threaten: "Your clause is illegal!" (hostile)
- Instead, educate: "Counsel will likely flag this. Here's the case law. Let's fix it now rather than in litigation."
