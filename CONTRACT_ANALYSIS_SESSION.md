## COMPARATIVE ANALYSIS: MAJOR SHIFTS

### 1. EQUITY MODEL - FUNDAMENTAL RESTRUCTURING

| Aspect | Timothy's Draft | Elizabeth's v3.6 | Impact |
|--------|-----------------|------------------|--------|
| **Model Type** | Simple Contingent | Two-Bucket Earned | Shifts risk to Timothy |
| **Baseline** | 0% → 30% on trigger | 0% throughout | Extends vesting window |
| **Vesting Speed** | Immediate on trigger | 2.5%/yr time + progressive results | Slower equity accrual |
| **Guarantee** | 30% if benchmark hit | Up to 30% (must earn) | No guarantee; performance-dependent |

**Analysis:** Elizabeth transforms a "contingent grant" into an "earned grant" system. This gives her control over the pace of equity accrual and creates multiple failure points for Timothy (rubric miss = time equity loss, low attribution = results equity loss).

### 2. REVENUE DEFINITION - ELIZABETH WINS

**Timothy's Draft:**
- $200K cumulative sales = cash received + outstanding billables
- Receivables counted up to 30% of total

**Elizabeth's v3.6:**
- $200K collected cash only
- Receivables never count toward trigger
- "Deemed credit" for improperly declined deals is equity-only, not cash commission

**Impact:** 
- Removes Timothy's ability to count pending revenue
- Creates risk: if customers pay slowly, trigger may never fire
- Eliminates cash compensation for declined deals (only equity credit)

### 3. COMMITMENT TIMING - NEW RISK FOR TIMOTHY

**Timothy's Draft:**
- Automatic upon trigger satisfaction

**Elizabeth's v3.6:**

**Impact:**
- Hard 90-day deadline creates timing risk
- Timothy might be unable to resign in 90 days (contractual obligations, non-compete)
- One day late = permanent 10% equity reduction

### 4. SALARY: 46% PAY CUT

**Timothy's Draft:** $100,000 annual base (fixed)

### 5. TIME BUCKET WITH 1-YEAR CLIFF - VESTING TRAP

**New Provision in v3.6:**

Elizabeth's model introduces time-based equity (10% bucket) with a 1-year cliff and 4-year vesting:
- **Cliff:** Depart before month 12 = lose ALL time equity earned
- **Vesting:** 2.5% per year for full-time (4 years to earn 10%)
- **Rubric Gate:** Must meet quarterly minimums (40 hrs/week, 50 prospects, 20 meetings, weekly updates)

**Impact:**
- Contradicts "earned is earned" principle
- Creates lock-in: Timothy must stay 12+ months to get any time equity
- Rubric scoring is subjective and controlled by Elizabeth

---

### 6. ATTRIBUTION SYSTEM - ELIZABETH CONTROLS 50% OF TIMOTHY'S UPSIDE

**New in v3.6:** Sophisticated 6-tier system that caps Timothy's credit on many deals

**Problem:**
- Infrastructure-sourced leads (Elizabeth's 221+ existing prospects) capped at 50% even if Timothy closes
- "Expert assistance" from Elizabeth doesn't reduce tier, but Elizabeth "taking over selling" makes it 50%
- Tagging is "joint" but Elizabeth is the operator/manager (she de facto controls tags)

**Example:**
- Deal from Elizabeth's database, Timothy closes = 50% credit = 0.5% equity vesting
- Same deal truly Timothy-sourced = 100% credit = 1% equity vesting
- Elizabeth's existing prospect list is ~221 prospects = most leads will be infrastructure-sourced

**Impact:**
- Most of Timothy's deals will be 50% tier or lower
- Average commission is likely 6-8%, not the stated 12%
- Results bucket earning is constrained by Elizabeth's existing infrastructure

---

### 7. COMMISSION IS LOW & ATTRIBUTION-DEPENDENT

**Calculation:**
- Commission = 12% × collected revenue × attribution tier
- Average attribution likely 50% (due to infrastructure-sourced leads)
- Average commission = 12% × 0.50 = 6% of revenue

**Example (Year 1):**
- $250K caused revenue
- $100K from Elizabeth's prospects (50% tier) = $100K × 0.50 = $50K credit = $6K commission
- $150K from Timothy's sourcing (100% tier) = $150K × 1.00 = $150K credit = $18K commission
- Total commission = $24K
- Salary = $54K
- **Total cash = $78K (below $100K promise)**

**Elizabeth's v3.6:**

## CRITICAL RED FLAGS FOR TIMOTHY

### 🚨 TIER 1 - DEAL BREAKERS

1. **90-Day Commitment Trap**
   - Hard deadline with equity step-down penalty
   - Timing risk outside Timothy's control
   - **Action:** Extend to 180 days or make automatic

2. **Salary Reduction ($100K → $54K)**
   - 46% pay cut is unsustainable
   - Stepping is revenue-dependent, not time-based
   - **Action:** Increase to $75K minimum; make steps time-based

3. **Attribution System**
   - Infrastructure-sourced cap at 50% is excessive
   - Timothy needs to source all new deals to get 100% credit
   - **Action:** Simplify to 100%/50%/0% and remove infrastructure cap

### ⚠️ TIER 2 - MAJOR CONCERNS

4. **Two-Bucket System Complexity**
   - Time bucket requires rubric compliance (hours, prospects, meetings, updates)
   - Results bucket contingent on deals closing
   - Timothy could work hard, miss one quarter of rubric, lose time equity
   - **Action:** Consolidate to single results bucket + fixed salary (no time equity accrual)

5. **1-Year Cliff on Time Equity**
   - Contradicts "earned is earned" principle
   - Forfeits all time equity if Timothy departs before month 12
   - **Action:** Remove cliff or make graduated (25%/50%/100% over 12 months)

6. **Indefinite Confidentiality**
   - No sunset = Timothy locked into knowledge restrictions for life
   - Covers conversational knowledge, not just written IP
   - **Action:** Add 3-5 year sunset post-departure

7. **Deemed Credit is Equity-Only**
   - If Elizabeth improperly declines a deal, Timothy gets equity, not cash commission
   - Equity doesn't pay bills
   - **Action:** Make deemed credit include cash commission at 50% of rate-card value

8. **WOSB Control Lock**
   - Elizabeth is sole manager; Timothy never gains operational control
   - Even at 30%, Timothy has zero voting power on major decisions
   - **Action:** Add path to co-management at 25%+ equity or require mutual decisions on pricing/hiring/product

9. **Vest-and-Dash Clawback**
   - If Timothy voluntarily resigns, Elizabeth can repurchase time equity at 25% discount
   - Penalizes leaving even if things aren't working
   - **Action:** Remove or make mutual (Elizabeth pays premium if she removes Timothy)

### ⚠️ TIER 3 - CONCERNS

10. **Rubric Scoring is Subjective**
    - "Logged data, never after-the-fact opinion" — but what counts as "qualified prospect"? "Meeting"?
    - Rubric-miss = loss of time equity that quarter
    - **Action:** Lock in specific, mechanical definitions for all rubric categories

11. **No Annual Renegotiation Trigger**
    - If market changes or revenue doesn't materialize, terms are locked in
    - **Action:** Add right to revisit terms annually or if projections missed

12. **License Reversion on Dissolution**
    - If company closes, license terminates and rights revert to Elizabeth
    - Timothy's equity becomes worthless
    - **Action:** License should survive dissolution and continue with any buyer/successor

## NEW HAMPSHIRE LAW CONSIDERATIONS

### Non-Compete Enforceability (RSA 275, etc.)

**Status in v3.6:**
- 12 months post-departure, narrowed to "direct competitors in website compliance scanning field"
- Extended while buyout installments are unpaid (up to 36 months total)
- Mutual non-compete (Elizabeth bound equally at 51%+ threshold)

**NH Law Requirements Met:**
✅ Reasonableness test (12 months is reasonable)
✅ Legitimate business interest (protecting client relationships, trade secrets)
✅ Not overbroad (narrowed to actual competitors, not general web design)
✅ No new-customer restriction explicitly avoided

**Concerns:**
⚠️ "Direct competitors" and "demonstrably planned products" need clearer definition
⚠️ Extended non-compete during buyout payment could be challenged
⚠️ Ensure counsel adds: "to the extent enforceable under NH law"

### IP Protection (RSA 304-C)

**Status in v3.6:**
- Core Engine = Elizabeth's personal property
- Company holds permanent, irrevocable, exclusive license
- License NOT terminable at either party's election
- Improvements belong to Elizabeth but auto-fall within license

**Analysis:**
✅ Non-terminable license protects Timothy's equity value
✅ Clean ownership structure (Elizabeth owns code, company owns products)
✅ License survives breakup/buyout

**Recommendation:** 
- Counsel should define "Core Engine," "Engine improvement," "product feature," and "company-funded development" precisely
- Ensure license terms cover all fields of use intended for both companies
- Add: If company is sold, buyer assumes all license rights

### Capital Contributions as Loans (RSA 304-C)

**Status in v3.6:**
- A's contributions documented as promissory notes
- Loans repaid before profit distributions
- Tax distributions paid before loan repayment

**Concern:**
⚠️ Elizabeth can "advance" herself unlimited salary if structured as loan
⚠️ Related-party compensation guardrail is CPA review, not hard cap

**Action:** Add related-party compensation cap (e.g., not to exceed 150% of industry-standard rate)

## CONCLUSION

Elizabeth's v3.6 is a **professionally drafted, legally sound, and heavily favorable-to-Elizabeth agreement**. It appears balanced through linguistic sophistication but:

1. **Reduces Timothy's cash compensation 46%** ($100K → $54K)
2. **Extends earning window** through two-bucket system and cliff provisions
3. **Constrains equity upside** through attribution system and infrastructure-sourced lead cap
4. **Locks Elizabeth's control** through WOSB structure and sole-manager provisions
5. **Creates compliance dependencies** through rubric scoring and quarterly measurement

**This is a "take it or walk away" offer from Elizabeth, dressed up as a fair partnership.**

Timothy must decide: Is a 15-18% equity stake + $54K salary + constrained upside worth the risk? Or should he renegotiate aggressively on Tier 1 issues?

If Elizabeth won't move on Tier 1, Timothy should **walk away** and pursue alternative opportunities. The deal as structured is not a fair partnership; it's employment with equity optionality.
