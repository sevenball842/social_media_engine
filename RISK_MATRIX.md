## SCENARIO 1: SALARY ($54K vs. $75K)

### IMPACT VECTOR: Cash Flow & Sustainability

| Scenario | Year 1 Salary | Year 1 Commission | Year 1 Total Cash | Runway | Risk |
|----------|--------------|------------------|-------------------|--------|------|
| **Accept $54K** | $54,000 | $12K-18K (est.) | $66K-72K | ~8-10 months if expenses $600/mo | ⚠️ HIGH |
| **Win $75K** | $75,000 | $12K-18K (est.) | $87K-93K | ~12-14 months if expenses $600/mo | ✅ ACCEPTABLE |
| **Compromise $65K** | $65,000 | $12K-18K (est.) | $77K-83K | ~10-12 months if expenses $600/mo | ⚠️ MEDIUM |

### Financial Analysis (4-Year Horizon):

**Scenario A: You Accept $54K**
```
Year 1:   $54K salary + $15K commission (avg) = $69K total
Year 2:   $54K salary + $25K commission (assuming $300K revenue) = $79K
Year 3:   $72K salary (hits $500K milestone) + $30K commission = $102K
Year 4:   $100K salary (hits $1M milestone) + $40K commission = $140K

4-Year Total Cash: $390K

Equity Vesting (Illustrative):
- Time bucket: ~8-10% (2.5% × 4 years, minus any missed quarters)
- Results bucket: ~8-12% (depending on attribution, $400-600K revenue)
- Total equity: ~16-22%

4-Year Total Value: $390K cash + 16-22% equity (~$100-200K at $1M valuation)
= $490-590K total compensation
```

**Scenario B: You Win $75K**
```
Year 1:   $75K salary + $15K commission = $90K total
Year 2:   $75K salary + $25K commission = $100K
Year 3:   $85K salary (steps at $500K) + $30K commission = $115K
Year 4:   $100K salary (steps at $1M) + $40K commission = $140K

4-Year Total Cash: $445K

Equity Vesting: (same as Scenario A, ~16-22%)

4-Year Total Value: $445K cash + 16-22% equity (~$100-200K)
= $545-645K total compensation
```

**Delta:** $75K salary = **$55K additional cash over 4 years** ($13.75K/year avg)

### Sustainability Risk Analysis:

**If Salary is $54K:**
- Assumes you have $15-20K in personal savings at start
- If product takes 18+ months to hit $200K trigger: you'll burn savings before full-time commitment
- Forces you into second income or debt during ramp-up
- Creates desperation/weakness in negotiations (you're financially stressed)
- **Moral hazard:** Elizabeth knows you're financially constrained; she can extract additional concessions later

**If Salary is $75K:**
- Sufficient to cover basic expenses in most US markets
- Allows focus on sales without second job
- Reduces financial stress and desperation
- You negotiate from position of strength (you have runway)

### Recommendation:
**Salary is non-negotiable at $75K minimum.** Anything less creates unsustainable cash flow and weakens your negotiating position in future disputes.

## SCENARIO 2: COMMITMENT WINDOW (90 Days vs. 180 Days)

### IMPACT VECTOR: Timing Risk & Flexibility

| Scenario | Window | Step-Down Penalty | Risk of Missing | Recovery Option |
|----------|--------|-------------------|-----------------| ---|
| **Accept 90-day hard stop** | 90 days calendar | -10% equity (30%→20%) | HIGH (60%+) | None; penalty is automatic |
| **Win 180-day window** | 180 days calendar | None (no step-down) | MEDIUM (15-20%) | No penalty even if you miss |
| **Compromise 120-day** | 120 days calendar | -5% equity (30%→25%) | MEDIUM-HIGH (30%) | Smaller penalty if missed |

### Realistic Constraint Analysis:

**Current Employment Barrier:**
- Your current employer likely requires 30-60 days notice
- Many employment agreements also include non-compete (prevents immediate pivot to Elizabeth's company)
- If you're non-competes from current job, you may need legal review (~1-2 weeks)
- Negotiating your exit (transition projects, knowledge transfer) may take additional 2-4 weeks

**Timeline Example (Realistic Scenario):**
```
Day 0:   $200K trigger fires; you decide to commit
Day 1-5: Give legal notice to current employer (written formal notice)
Day 5:   Current employer triggers waiting period (30-60 days)
Day 5:   You begin legal review of non-compete restrictions (2-4 weeks)
Day 20:  Legal review complete; you determine you can start at Elizabeth
Day 35:  30-day notice expires (if that's what employer requires)
Day 45:  You're ready to resign and join Elizabeth

Total time: 45 days (within 90-day window, but barely)
```

**If Your Current Job Has Non-Compete:**
```
Day 0:   Trigger fires
Day 1-5: Legal review of non-compete (can you work for Elizabeth's competitor?)
Day 20:  Non-compete may prevent you from joining Elizabeth (same market)
Day 20-45: Negotiate non-compete release or settlement with current employer
Day 45+: Potentially unable to join within 90 days

Result: You miss 90-day window; lose 10% equity through no fault of your own
```

### Probability Analysis:

**Probability of Meeting 90-Day Deadline:**
- If current job has no non-compete: 70-80% chance you meet it
- If current job has standard non-compete: 30-40% chance you meet it
- If current job requires legal negotiation: 20-30% chance you meet it

**Your Risk Exposure:**
- 60-70% chance you MISS the 90-day deadline (due to external constraints)
- If you miss: you lose $10-50K in equity value (10% of $100-500K valuation)
- **This is a ~$50K+ risk for something outside your control**

### Recovery/Mitigation Options:

**Option A: Accept 180-Day Window (My Proposal)**
- Realistic timeline for anyone with current employment constraints
- No step-down penalty
- You control the timing

**Option B: Accept 120-Day Window with 5% Step-Down**
- Compromise between your constraint and Elizabeth's timeline
- Smaller penalty (5% vs. 10%) if you miss
- Still accounts for employment law constraints

**Option C: Automatic Commitment Trigger**
- No decision window at all
- Commitment automatic upon $200K revenue
- You give notice "as soon as legally possible"
- No penalty for realistic employment constraints

### Recommendation:
**Push for 180-day window or automatic trigger.** The 90-day hard stop exposes you to $50K+ risk for events outside your control (employment contract constraints, non-compete negotiation delays). This is the **worst risk/reward tradeoff in the entire agreement**.

## SCENARIO 3: ATTRIBUTION SYSTEM (6 Tiers vs. 3 Tiers)

### IMPACT VECTOR: Commission Upside & Equity Ceiling

| Scenario | Avg Attribution | Year 1 Revenue | Year 1 Commission | Equity Upside | Risk |
|----------|-----------------|-----------------|-------------------|---------------|------|
| **Accept 6-tier + 50% infrastructure cap** | 45-50% | $250K caused | $11-13K commission | Capped at 12% (due to low attribution) | ⚠️ HIGH |
| **Win 3-tier (100/50/0)** | 65-70% | $250K caused | $16-18K commission | ~14-16% equity | ✅ ACCEPTABLE |
| **Compromise 4-tier** | 55-60% | $250K caused | $13-15K commission | ~13-15% equity | ✅ ACCEPTABLE |

### Detailed Commission Analysis:

**Scenario A: Accept 6-Tier + Infrastructure Cap**

Assumption: In Year 1, you cause $250K in revenue
- $100K from Elizabeth's existing prospects (infrastructure-sourced, capped 50%)
- $150K from entirely new leads (100% tier)

```
Revenue Attribution Breakdown:
- $100K × 50% = $50K attributed (infrastructure cap)
- $150K × 100% = $150K attributed (your sourcing)
- Total attributed: $200K

Commission Calculation (12% of attributed × collected):
- $50K × 12% = $6K commission (infrastructure deals)
- $150K × 12% = $18K commission (your deals)
- Total commission: $24K

BUT if infrastructure deals are tagged more aggressively:
- $130K from infrastructure (Elizabeth tags deals as "her lead" more aggressively)
- $120K from your sourcing
- Total attributed: $200K (same revenue, different split)
- Commission: $15.6K + $14.4K = $30K vs. $24K above

Difference: $6K (26% variance) depends on how Elizabeth tags deals
```

**Scenario B: Win 3-Tier Simplified (100/50/0)**

Same Year 1 scenario:
- All "new" deals you source: 100% tier
- All deals from Elizabeth's database that you re-engage and close: 50% tier
- Deals Elizabeth found and closed entirely: 0% tier

```
Revised Attribution (with objective tagging):
- $100K deals: 50% tier (Elizabeth's prospects, you closed) = $50K attributed
- $150K deals: 100% tier (you sourced) = $150K attributed
- Total attributed: $200K

Commission:
- $50K × 12% = $6K (Elizabeth prospects tier)
- $150K × 12% = $18K (your sourcing tier)
- Total commission: $24K

Key difference: You ALWAYS get 100% attribution on deals you truly source.
Under 6-tier system, Elizabeth can tag a deal as 75% or 50% even if you sourced it.
Under 3-tier system, you get 100% unless Elizabeth's lead/prospect.
```

### Equity Ceiling Impact:

**With 6-Tier System (50% avg attribution):**
```
Year 1: $250K caused revenue × 50% avg attribution = $125K attributed to results bucket
→ 2.5% equity (capped at 20% results bucket)

Year 2: Assume $400K caused revenue × 45% avg attribution = $180K attributed
→ 3.6% equity

Year 3: Assume $600K caused revenue × 50% avg attribution = $300K attributed
→ 6% equity

Year 4: Assume $1M caused revenue × 50% avg attribution = $500K attributed
→ 10% equity (hits 20% results ceiling)

Total results equity: ~22% (but capped at 20%, so 20% achieved)
Plus time equity: ~8-10%
Total equity at Year 4: 28-30%
```

**With 3-Tier System (65% avg attribution):**
```
Year 1: $250K caused revenue × 65% avg attribution = $162.5K attributed
→ 3.25% equity

Year 2: $400K caused revenue × 65% avg attribution = $260K attributed
→ 5.2% equity

Year 3: $600K caused revenue × 70% avg attribution = $420K attributed
→ 8.4% equity

Year 4: $1M caused revenue × 70% avg attribution = $700K attributed
→ 14% equity (hits 20% results ceiling)

Total results equity: ~31% (capped at 20%, so 20% achieved)
Plus time equity: ~8-10%
Total equity at Year 4: 28-30%
```

**Net Difference:** With 3-tier system, you hit the 20% results ceiling earlier and with higher confidence.

### Subjectivity/Dispute Risk Analysis:

**6-Tier System Dispute Scenarios:**

1. **Deal: Acme Corp ($100K contract)**
   - You: "I found Acme in LinkedIn search, cold-called them, did all selling"
   - Elizabeth: "Acme was in my database from 2025; that's infrastructure-sourced (50%)"
   - Dispute: 100% vs. 50% tier = 0.5% equity difference = $5K commission difference
   - Resolution: 5-day tag resolution window (Section 17.1); if unresolved, arbitration ($3K+ cost)
   - **You probably can't afford to fight; you concede**

2. **Deal: Beta Corp ($75K contract)**
   - You: "You closed the technical presentation, so that's 75% tier"
   - Elizabeth: "I did close the presentation, but you did 95% of the selling; that's more like 100%"
   - Dispute: 75% vs. 100% tier = 1.875% equity difference = $1.875K commission
   - Resolution: 5-day tag resolution; if unresolved, arbitration
   - **Same dynamic: hard to afford dispute resolution**

**3-Tier System Dispute Scenarios (Simpler):**

1. **Deal: Acme Corp ($100K contract)**
   - You: "I found this; 100% tier"
   - Elizabeth: "You're right; it's 100%"
   - Or: "I provided the lead; 50% tier"
   - Dispute: 100% vs. 50% tier = clear binary choice
   - Much easier to define "your lead" vs. "their sourcing"

2. **Deal: Beta Corp ($75K contract)**
   - Clear question: Did you source it or did Elizabeth?
   - If split effort: use 50% tier (objective)
   - Much less subjectivity than 6-tier system

**Dispute Cost Analysis:**

- 6-Tier System: High dispute frequency (subjective tiers), high cost (can't afford arbitration)
- 3-Tier System: Low dispute frequency (objective), lower cost (binary decisions)

### Recommendation:
**Push hard for 3-tier simplified system.** The 6-tier system:
1. Gives Elizabeth subjective control over your commission
2. Creates high dispute risk (but you can't afford to resolve disputes)
3. Reduces your effective commission by 15-20%
4. Is vulnerable to challenge as "overly subjective" under NH law

The 3-tier system is **more favorable to you AND more likely to be enforceable.**

## WALK-AWAY ANALYSIS

### When to Walk Away:

1. **Elizabeth Won't Move on Salary**
   - If she insists on $54K and won't negotiate to $70K+: **WALK**
   - Reason: $54K signals she's not serious about you; she's optimizing for low cash burn
   - $54K = desperation wage; you can't sustain on it

2. **Elizabeth Won't Move on Commitment Window**
   - If she keeps 90-day hard stop + 10% step-down: **WALK**
   - Reason: $50K equity risk for calendar timing is unreasonable
   - This signals she doesn't trust you to commit; partnership starts adversarially

3. **Elizabeth Won't Simplify Attribution**
   - If she keeps 6-tier + infrastructure cap and refuses to simplify: **RECONSIDER**
   - Reason: You lose 15-20% of commission upside; she controls your earnings
   - This is manageable **only if she concedes on salary and commitment window**

### Best Walk-Away Scenario:

```
Elizabeth refuses to move on all 3 Tier 1 items + keeps legal red flags (indefinite 
confidentiality, 36-month non-compete, vest-and-dash clawback).

→ Partnership is not viable.
→ You walk away.
→ You pursue alternative opportunities (other startups, consulting, etc.)
```

### Acceptable Walk-Away Scenario:

```
Elizabeth moves on Tier 1 items (concedes on salary and commitment window), but 
refuses to simplify attribution system.

→ Partnership may still be viable.
→ You can manage the 6-tier system if salary is $70K+ and window is 120+ days.
→ You negotiate Tier 2 issues (confidentiality sunset, non-compete cap, vest-and-dash removal).
```
